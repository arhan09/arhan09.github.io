Arhan Boyd

Incoming PhD, Information Systems & Analytics, National University of Singapore
Research Associate, Indian School of Business

I am a microeconomist working on the economics of information systems. In January 2027 I begin a PhD in Information Systems & Analytics at the School of Computing, National University of Singapore, supported by the NUS Research Scholarship.

I am currently a Research Associate in Information Systems at the Indian School of Business, where I work with Ravi Bapna and Vandith Pamuru.

I trained as an economist at the Barcelona School of Economics, where I took a master’s in Specialized Economic Analysis (Competition, Regulation and Markets), and at the University of Queensland, where I read economics with a major in quantitative analysis and mathematics.

Most of my work is a question of identification: what a digital market is actually doing, and whether it can be recovered from the traces it leaves. My master’s thesis modelled the welfare effects of data privacy regulation through a Cournot framework, and privacy remains one of the questions I keep returning to.

Research interests

  • Economics of information systems
  • Causal inference and applied microeconometrics
  • Economics of privacy and data regulation
  • Algorithmic correction, at the intersection of information systems and computer science
  • Agentic AI and its economic implications
  • Digital platforms, payments, and online marketplaces
  • LLMs and NLP as measurement tools in economics
